FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to what most buyers ask before they decide.

Is this financial advice? +

No. This is an educational decision-support tool. It models the math — mortgage, taxes, equity, appreciation, investment returns, exit costs — using your inputs and standard formulas, then shows the result. It does not know your full tax situation, life plans, or risk tolerance. For a personal recommendation, talk to a qualified financial advisor.

Is it really free? What's the catch? +

Yes — the full tool, every feature, no paywall and no "pro" tier. There's no catch: the whole thing runs in your browser, so there's nothing to host, store, or charge you for. If it helps you make a better decision, share it with someone else who's about to sign.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere? +

No. Every calculation runs in your browser. Your home price, income, debts, and other inputs never leave your device — we don't have a database storing them, and there's nothing for us to lose if we got hacked.

Do I need to create an account? +

No account, no password, no email signup. Open the tool, enter your numbers, and get your answer. Want to save or share an analysis? The Share button copies a link that reproduces it exactly on any device.

How is this different from Zillow / Rocket / NerdWallet calculators? +

Most calculators answer "what's my monthly payment?" — they show P&I, maybe taxes/insurance, and stop. This tool answers a different question: "will buying this home leave me wealthier than renting and investing the difference?" It models 5–10 years of equity build, appreciation, exit costs, and what renters earn by investing the cash they don't tie up in a down payment. The output is a recommendation with a confidence level, not just a number.

What loan types does it support? +

Conventional, FHA, VA, and Jumbo. Mortgage insurance is auto-calculated based on loan type and down payment — FHA gets MIP, conventional under 20% down gets PMI, VA has no monthly MI, and so on. You can override the loan type explicitly if you want to test different scenarios.

Will I get the same answer if my assumptions change? +

That's exactly what the sensitivity analysis is for. The tool tells you how far rates can move, or how much home prices can change, before the recommendation flips. If you're "barely buy," it says so — and shows you which inputs you should sanity-check before signing.
